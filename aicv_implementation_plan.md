**Step 2: Discovery Call (You)**

**Step 3: Onboarding (Automated)**
- Payment via Stripe ($500 deposit + balance before delivery)

**Step 4: Production (India Resource)**

## 6. RISK MITIGATION

### Operational Risks
**Risk**: India resource quality inconsistent
**Mitigation**: Start with 2-3 vetted freelancers, detailed brief templates, QA checklist

**Risk**: Video recording complexity
**Mitigation**: Provide detailed recording guide, offer DIY option with phone, budget for re-shoots

**Risk**: Chatbot accuracy issues
**Mitigation**: Use established frameworks (LangChain + OpenAI), test extensively, include disclaimers

### Market Risks
**Risk**: Price resistance
**Mitigation**: Start with 2-3 beta clients at $997, collect testimonials, then raise prices

**Risk**: Long sales cycle
**Mitigation**: Offer $500 deposit to start, flexible payment plans, money-back guarantee

**Risk**: Competitors emerge
**Mitigation**: Build strong brand, focus on service quality, develop proprietary templates
